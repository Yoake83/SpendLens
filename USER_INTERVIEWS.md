# User Interviews

Three real conversations with potential users. 10-15 minutes each.
Conducted during the week of the assignment.

---

## How to find people fast (do this today)

**Cold DM on X/Twitter:**
> "Hey [name] — building a free AI spend audit tool for startup engineering teams.
> Takes 10 min to chat — would love your perspective on how you think about AI tool costs.
> Happy to share the tool early as a thank you."

**Indie Hackers Slack #general:**
> "Anyone on a startup team paying for Cursor, Copilot, or Claude?
> Building a spend audit tool, looking to talk to 3 people for 10 min.
> Drop a reply or DM me."

**Your college network:**
> Message anyone working at a startup who uses AI tools day-to-day.

Target profile: engineering manager, CTO, or senior dev at a 5-50 person startup.
