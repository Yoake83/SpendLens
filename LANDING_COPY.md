## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free? What's the catch?**
A: Yes, completely free. SpendLens is built by Credex, which sells discounted AI credits.
If your audit shows significant savings, we'll mention Credex as one way to capture more.
No paywall, no credit card.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: No. Enter your tools, get your audit, optionally share your email to receive the report.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every price is sourced from the vendor's official pricing page and verified during
submission week. Sources are cited in our methodology. We update monthly.

**Q: What if I disagree with a recommendation?**
A: The audit engine makes general recommendations based on plan features and team size.
The reasoning is shown for every recommendation — disagree if your situation is different.

**Q: Is my data stored or shared?**
A: Your audit data is stored to power the shareable link. Your email (if provided) is
used only to send the report and is not included in the public URL. We don't sell data.
